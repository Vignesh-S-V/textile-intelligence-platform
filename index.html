Methodology & Data Sources

Forecasting Methodology

The forecast engine evaluates multiple time-series approaches and automatically selects the best-performing model for the active filter selection using rolling historical backtesting and Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE).

  • Adaptive Holt — level and trend exponential smoothing.
  • Damped Trend — trend extrapolation with damping to reduce long-horizon overstatement.
  • Linear Trend — least-squares trend projection.
  • Drift — historical average change extrapolation.
  • Recent Mean — average of the most recent observations.
  • Seasonal Naive — prior-year monthly pattern when at least 24 monthly observations exist.
  • Seasonal-Trend Ensemble — weighted combination of seasonal and trend forecasts.

Forecast accuracy is backtested on the active filtered series. A 95% result is treated as a validation target, not a guaranteed outcome. Forecasts are withheld when the selected filter has fewer than six monthly observations.

Price Data Sources

  • TXC — Office of the Textile Commissioner: official historical/weekly cotton yarn price publications. Official Price Reports
  • NTC — National Textile Corporation Limited: regional yarn price lists, including Southern and Western regions. Official NTC Website
  • NHDC — National Handloom Development Corporation: official yarn rate publications for handloom-related yarn categories. Official Yarn Rates
  • SIMA — Southern India Mills' Association: public e-review and textile market publications used as association-market indicators where explicit yarn prices are published. SIMA Textile Data
  • TEA — Tiruppur Exporters' Association: public industry/news publications used as association-market indicators when explicit yarn prices are stated. TEA News & Publications

Source classification: TXC, NTC and NHDC records are retained as official source records. SIMA and TEA observations are clearly classified as association-market indicators and are not represented as government-official prices. No missing-month interpolation or previous-month price copying is performed.
