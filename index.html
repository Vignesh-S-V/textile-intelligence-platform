Forecast method

The engine aggregates exact filtered observations into monthly averages, compares Adaptive Holt, Damped Trend, Linear Trend, Drift, Recent Mean and Seasonal Naive when sufficient history exists, and selects the lowest rolling-backtest MAPE model. A current market indication may be used as a clearly labelled anchor when a market is explicitly selected.
